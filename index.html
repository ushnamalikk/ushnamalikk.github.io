About

I study how AI-mediated systems shape information access, judgment, and decision-making.

My interests span human–AI interaction, AI-mediated search, algorithmic auditing, and computational social science. Before Iowa I completed my BSc at LUMS, working with Ihsan Ayyub Qazi, Ayesha Ali, and Zafar Ayyub Qazi.
